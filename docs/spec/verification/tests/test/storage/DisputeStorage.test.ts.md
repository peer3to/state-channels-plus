## Overview

The suite drives `DisputeStorage` directly with factory-built signed disputes and co-signatures
made by fresh wallets over the dispute hash. It covers `storeDispute` and
`storeDisputeConfirmation` under computed and caller-provided hashes, `getDisputeConfirmation`
reads, and the retention rule: the first copy is stored with exactly its signatures, later `SYNC`
copies (once or repeatedly, with extra signatures) leave it unchanged, a `CHAIN_EVENT` copy
replaces a `SYNC` copy, a `SYNC` copy after a `CHAIN_EVENT` copy and a second `CHAIN_EVENT` copy are
ignored, different hashes stay independent, and a later copy under the same hash keeps the original
signed dispute. The oracles assert the returned hash, the stored signed dispute's identity, and the
exact stored signature list. The per-fork flags are not exercised here, and no test re-delivers an
identical complete confirmation or decodes the stored dispute, so those permutations stay
unassigned.
