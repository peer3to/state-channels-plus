## Overview

Queue or restore factory-built copies of the same block and inspect the resulting timestamp; undefined preserves and defined zero replaces.

The suite drives `QueueStorage` directly (and twice through the `Storage` facade proxy) with
factory-built blocks and random signatures: queueing and merging copies with overlapping
signature sets, copy-scoped sender attribution, the structural source cap acting as an overflow
marker under honest-first and junk-first floods, exact-coordinate and lowest-height priority
dequeues, two competing bodies at one coordinate, restoring a dequeued entry with attribution
and the original `firstSeenAt` intact, and deep-copy isolation of queued and dequeued objects.
Oracles inspect the queued entry itself (`getQueuedEntry`: merged set, `sourcePeers`,
`signatureSources`, `overflowedSources`) and the dequeue results. The timestamp tests pin the
current overwrite behavior rather than the spec's earliest-wins merge, and no test permutes
arrival orders, delivers a duplicate copy with a signature-set oracle, clears a fork, or shows
priority dequeue leaving other forks untouched, so those permutations stay unassigned.
