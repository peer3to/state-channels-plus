## Overview

Real sessions exercise timeout checks, accepted-block and membership guards, and timer scheduling. Named contract failures at send and receipt re-enter the real scheduler, with later submissions forwarded to chain. Controlled block arrival, disposal and verified synchronization exercise obsolete checks. Private entry is limited to `createTimeOutDispute` (placing a real early timeout before its local deadline) and `tryTimeoutParticipant` (driving a second mismatch round, and the deadline helper); the normal recheck is the behavior under test. The disposal case retains the real inline host endpoint, runs the held retry after abort and checks its submission record after root cleanup; it sends no query through a closed port. Previous-producer posting-state refusals in either boolean direction re-enter the same scheduler with a fixed one-second recheck under a distinct reason, at send and at receipt and across consecutive refusals; replaced forks, disposal and accepted writer blocks obsolete the retry. The writer-block mismatch case also requires that the refused claim is no longer stored and that a later dispute on the fork carries no timeout. A predecessor marked posted only in the observer's local view must yield a claim with the chain's posting state that commits without a refusal. The validation retry is reached through public `scheduleCheck` while the predecessor's recovered validation is pending.
