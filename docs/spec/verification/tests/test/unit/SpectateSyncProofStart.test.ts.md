# SpectateSyncProofStart.test.ts

Test file: [test/unit/SpectateSyncProofStart.test.ts](../../../../../../test/unit/SpectateSyncProofStart.test.ts)

## Overview

Synchronizes peers against differing proof starts and final points. Cases check local-to-chain fallback, compact history, persisted final state and safe replay boundaries. A group of cases serves a requester that holds its own final point a proof that verifies only from a start below it: a crafted tail or threshold-final block conflicting with that final point, the colluders' bypass hop that skips a seated joiner's JOIN, and an honest continuation over a top-up its local diamond missed; they read the walk answers, installs, replays, the local final point, the rejections, the blacklist and the kept sync evidence, then release the missed log or post a later chain anchor and apply the next answer. The reverse-direction cases serve participant 2 a threshold-final block at its final height that consumes a later joiner's JOIN its own final point lacks: the sync declines, keeps the proof as sync evidence, rejects and blacklists nobody, sets no dispute marker, and the commit step refuses to sign its latest block; a later honest answer from peer 0 that verifies from its final point leaves the stop in place. The equal-inbound conflict case reads the same signing state and finds it unchanged. S2 counts a second trusted-start walk: the observer's build of its own proof for the reverse check. Two cases serve a proof that ends below the requester's own final point, whose pass from that final point checks nothing: participant 2 gets that JOIN-consuming block one below its final point (walked from the anchor only the reverse regression kill holds: evidence kept, signing stopped, nobody blamed), and Alice of the seated-joiner history gets, through a spectator relay, a block conflicting with the lower hop that seated Charlie (the conflict kill holds: evidence kept, the relay not blacklisted).
