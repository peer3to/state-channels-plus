## Responsibility and observable boundary

The disputer role's on-chain arm: this file constructs and submits disputes and kills invalid ones.
It owns Stages 1–2 of the [dispute pipeline algorithm](../../../../specification/disputes/dispute-processing.md)
(escalation guard, construction, atomic submission) plus the kill submission of Stage 5. It does
**not** audit disputes (`DisputeValidationService`), reduce windows (`ReductionManager`/`Executor`),
or observe chain events (`EventHandler`) — those callers drive it.

Public surface: [`dispute(forkId)`](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L105)
(escalate a fork, idempotent per fork),
[`killDispute(dispute)`](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L457) (submit the
stored dispute fraud proof during the kill period),
[`constructDispute(forkId)`](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L552) (build
the complete `Dispute` + confirmation + auditing data + proofs-to-apply from the proof
`AgreementManager.buildStateProof` returns; also used by the auditor's evidence comparison),
[`getAuditingData(forkId, proof, options?)`](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L779)
(assemble the audit payload of a built proof or of a foreign `StateProof`, reporting `isPartial`
when local data cannot back it; the finalized state is the state of the snapshot the chain's walk
ends at; the outbound message range starts at the proof start's outbound tip),
[`shouldAddOwnEvidence(forkId, dispute)`](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L365) and
[`forgetEvidenceComparison(forkId)`](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L453) (the auditor's once-per-fork evidence comparison,
called by `EventHandler`), and the `setChannelId`/`setP2pEventHooks` wiring setters.

## Key design decisions

Admission checks the live fork under the state mutex before construction and marker assignment. Detached requests use the shared observer; an early refusal carrying a zero-address timeout schedules no participant retry. See [DisputeManager.ts](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L126).

Error text delegates to the dependency-free errorMessage helper. Existing catch policy, stack fields, log messages and error propagation remain at this call site. See [DisputeManager.ts](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L25).

1. **Once-per-fork guard with rollback, under a local mutex.** `dispute()` serializes on the
   instance mutex and consults the `didIDispute` flag; the flag is set _before_ awaiting the
   transaction and rolled back on any submission failure, so a raced or reverted upload releases
   the guard for retry ([#L116](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L116),
   [#L273](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L273)). The mutex bounds
   concurrent local escalations; the _contract's_ throttle and one-post-per-window rules remain the
   authority.
2. **Fraud proofs ride in front of the upload, atomically.** Held proofs for unslashed participants
   are applied and the dispute uploaded in one batched call
   ([#L178](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L178)), so the dispute's
   claimed `onChainSlashes` are a subset of the on-chain set at execution — the mechanism that keeps
   fraud enforcement separate from reduction.
3. **The dispute pre-commits to its own outcome via the mirror.** The successor-fork genesis is
   computed by the mirrored canonical logic
   (`computeDisputeOutputSnapshotData.staticCall`,
   [#L694](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L694)) and its hash embedded as
   `outputSnapshotDataHash` — never a client-side reimplementation
   ([`INV-MIRROR-1-VAF778` (Single implementation)](../../../../specification/enforcement/local-mirror.md#inv-mirror-1-vaf778)).
4. **Data-availability decision by finality probe, local first.** Auditing calldata is posted
   unless the proof's last milestone is provably final to everyone (`isLastMilestoneFinalByEveryone.staticCall`,
   [#L728-L739](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L728-L739)); a known-final last milestone spares the calldata cost. The probe runs
   through `preferLocal` on the local diamond: a local "not final" posts the data without asking
   the chain, because posting is never wrong, only costlier; a local "final" omits the data, which
   is slashable if the lagging mirror was wrong, so the chain confirms that answer and decides
   ([`REQ-MIRROR-4-H9C4YS` (Local-first evaluation, adverse answer confirmed)](../../../../specification/enforcement/local-mirror.md#req-mirror-4-h9c4ys)). A local failure of the probe (EVM revert, executor or worker
   failure) propagates: construction throws with no chain read. The on-chain predicate judges the chain snapshot set, which cannot move
   while a proof can land (adoption targets only the latest undisputed fork, a disputed fork advances only by
   reduction), plus the joiners at or below the dispute's inbound anchor, minus the dispute's own `onChainSlashes`, so
   this construction-time probe and a later fraud-proof read of the committed dispute agree.
5. **Construction aborts on partial data.** `getAuditingData` flags any locally unbackable element
   (`isPartial`) and `constructDispute` throws `PartialAuditingDataError` rather than submitting a
   dispute the node cannot stand behind ([#L657-L660](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L657-L660)).
6. **Kill runs without the mutex, on a single-audit invariant.** `killDispute` relies on the audit
   producing exactly one dispute fraud proof per dispute exactly once (pipeline [`INV-DVP-3-ZMF1HA`](../../../views/architecture/sdk/dispute-pipeline.md#inv-dvp-3-zmf1ha)), so no
   local serialization is needed; it re-reads the kill window before preparing gas, checks after
   the gas estimate (its last await before the send) that the dispute's fork is still the active
   fork and otherwise returns without sending, and treats the four named race reverts as benign
   convergence
   ([#L470-L481](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L470-L481), [#L488-L497](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L488-L497), [#L514-L539](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L514-L539)).
7. **Construction consumes the observed slash subset; a conditional refusal refreshes it.**
   The construction result records that observation. After `RaceConditionDisputeWindowNotOpen`,
   marker rollback and mutex release precede authoritative event recovery. Normal construction runs
   again only if the chain set changed since construction, including a slash already delivered in
   the meantime. Construction keeps the existing participant eligibility filter; a slash is not
   automatically a valid reason.
8. **Gas from the chain signer; replays funded upfront.** The plain upload and the calldata upload
   pass no gas limit; the chain signer sends each with its estimate plus
   [`withGasHeadroom`](../../utils/gas.ts.md). Honest peers race to dispute the same fraud, and a late
   disputer's upload costs more for every disputer already in the window
   (`_hadParticipantPostedEvidence` reads the whole `hasPosted` list). A dispute landing between
   estimate and inclusion would leave a bare estimate short; the out-of-gas revert surfaces only as
   the proxy's "Delegatecall failed", and the retry can land at `evidencePeriodEnd`, refusing an
   honest disputer's evidence.
9. **StateManager orders admitted signing before the single dispute marker.**
   Dispute admission waits for the state mutex, sets the marker after admitted signatures and storage
   complete, then releases that mutex before construction and network submission. Callers already
   inside the state mutex use observed detached requests. Failed uploads roll back the marker;
   later same-fork block work is allowed again. No second closed-fork set or post-signature check is used.
10. **An inbound-head refusal is not retried.** `RaceConditionDisputeInboundNotLatest` means the
    dispute's anchor is behind the chain's inbound head at upload. Local storage is assumed current
    through event sync, so this is treated as a lost race like any other unhandled revert: the marker
    rolls back and the attempt ends.
11. **A transaction that replays a transition is funded for the replay upfront.** The state
    machine refuses to run a transition it cannot grant its full budget
    ([AStateMachine](../../contracts/V1/AStateMachine.sol.md)), and the manager turns that refusal
    into a failed call, not a verdict. The two sends that can replay a transition — the fraud-proof
    `multicall` in front of an upload ([#L178-L187](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L178-L187)) and `applyDisputeFraudProofs` in
    `killDispute` ([#L483-L502](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L483-L502)) — pass `gasLimit = replayGasLimit(estimate)`: the
    signer's estimate (the chain signer's `estimateGas` already adds
    [`withGasHeadroom`](../../utils/gas.ts.md)) plus `getStateTransitionReplayGas()`
    ([#L343-L357](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L343-L357)). The refusal checks that the full budget is free when the replay
    starts. An estimator that reports the gas spent (the peer3 hardhat fork the poker consumer runs
    executes the transaction once and adds 30%) counts only what the transition used, never the
    unused budget that must be free, and the work before the replay (proof checks, setting the
    machine's state, deleting the previous transition's outbound messages) can exceed any fixed margin over the requirement. On a poker dispute that work
    was about 3.9M gas with a 5.64M requirement, so about 9.5M was needed, while the larger of
    the estimate and the requirement plus a 30% margin gives only 8.89M. The sum
    covers both parts. A
    searching estimator (geth, anvil, and the SDK's own test node, hardhat 2.22 with EDR) looks for
    the lowest gas at which the whole transaction succeeds; the refusal makes every lower gas fail,
    so its estimate already contains the requirement and the sum declares more than needed (at
    least about 2.5 × the requirement). That changes no verdict but is not free: it reserves block
    space and must fit the block gas limit (limits section). Engineer decision (Luka, review 5 FO1):
    keep the sum; the whole gas and estimation topic is open as [`FIND-GASEST-2-94YFZ6`](../../../../audit/open-findings.md#find-gasest-2-94yfz6). Both estimates are taken through `getFunction`, the contract's own method. The replay gas
    is read once and kept as a promise, so concurrent sends share one read; a failed read is dropped
    and the next send reads again ([#L344-L354](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L344-L354)). No other send passes a gas limit
    ([`REQ-ENFSM-1-DKJCY2` (Injected context, bounded gas)](../../../../specification/enforcement/execution-and-consumer.md#req-enfsm-1-dkjcy2)).
12. **The auditor's evidence comparison is dispute policy and lives here.** After a valid audit of
    another participant's dispute, `EventHandler` asks `shouldAddOwnEvidence(forkId, dispute)`
    ([#L365-L390](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L365-L390)) whether this node should add its own dispute to the window. A
    node that already disputed the fork (`storage.disputes.didIDispute`) has committed its
    evidence: that check runs first and nothing is compared ([#L369](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L369)). Otherwise
    `canConstructMoreEvidence` builds this node's own dispute with `constructDispute` and compares
    `reduce([theirs])` with `reduce([ours, theirs])` on the local diamond ([#L396-L447](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L396-L447)).
    The comparison runs once per disputed fork and its promise is kept in `evidenceChecks`
    ([#L72](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L72), [#L387](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L387)): reduction merges evidence monotonically, so a dispute of
    ours that adds nothing to the first audited dispute adds nothing once more disputes land.
    Concurrent audits share the in-flight promise. A rejected comparison is removed and the
    rejection reaches that audit; a comparison ended by partial own auditing data
    (`PartialAuditingDataError`, answered `undefined` at [#L401-L415](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L401-L415)) is no answer: it
    is removed and counts as `false` for that audit only ([#L373-L386](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L373-L386)). Removal checks
    promise identity, so a comparison that settles after it was dropped cannot erase its
    replacement. A positive answer stays, so a failed upload is retried by the next audit.
    `forgetEvidenceComparison(forkId)` ([#L453-L455](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L453-L455)) drops the kept answer; `EventHandler`
    calls it on a kill (the compared dispute may be gone) and when the fork's reduced result is
    committed (memory pruning only). The comparison gates only joining another participant's
    dispute: new own evidence (fraud, timeout, leave) is disputed through its own paths
    ([`REQ-DISPUTE-PIPE-6-6FZB9M` (Minimal intervention and convergence)](../../../../specification/disputes/dispute-processing.md#req-dispute-pipe-6-6fzb9m)).
13. **One owner builds the own proof; the outbound range starts at the proof start.**
    `constructDispute` builds its proof with one `agreementManager.buildStateProof(forkId, latestBlockHeight)`
    call, read in parallel with the latest snapshot, the mirror's slash set and the participants
    ([#L558-L587](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L558-L587), call at [#L564](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L564)). It passes the whole `BuiltStateProof` to
    `getAuditingData` ([#L652-L656](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L652-L656)) and puts its `stateProof` in the dispute input
    ([#L676](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L676)). This file holds no proof-start rule and no proof-assembly logic:
    `AgreementManager` picks the local proof start (the same-fork non-genesis on-chain snapshot on
    the local diamond, else the fork genesis), assembles the compact proof and walks it once.
    `getAuditingData` uses the proof start's `latestOutboundMessageBlockHash` as the lower bound of
    the outbound message range ([#L877-L886](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L877-L886)). Why: a peer that installed the fork from an
    on-chain snapshot holds no outbound blocks below it, and the exits below the proof start are
    already on chain. With the genesis bound it would flag the auditing data `isPartial`, and its
    dispute would be refused or killed ([`REQ-SP-8-9PK9TS`](../../../../specification/disputes/dispute-processing.md#req-sp-8-9pk9ts)).
    When the proof start is the fork genesis, the range is the full one. `getAuditingData` still
    requires the fork genesis snapshot ([#L791-L796](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L791-L796)), because the auditing data carries its
    data; whether that dependency can go once the peer has moved ahead on the fork is open
    ([`OQ-SPEC-GENESIS-1-TKNMPM` (Fork genesis dependency after a peer moves ahead)](../../../../specification/open-questions.md#oq-spec-genesis-1-tknmpm)).
14. **No on-chain check of the own proof; a build failure propagates.** `constructDispute` does
    not verify its proof on chain before signing (no `verifyStateProof` or structure read) and never
    rebuilds it. `getAuditingData` reads the chain's walk of the proof only to pick the finalized
    state (decision 15); an invalid walk there gives `"0x"` and does not stop the construction. A throw from
    `buildStateProof` (height below the local proof start, a missing participant-change block, a
    missing or unlinked unfinal-tail block) rejects the parallel read, is logged as
    `Error constructing dispute - failed to get inputData` and is rethrown unchanged
    ([#L576-L587](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L576-L587)). `dispute()` takes it in its generic catch: it logs `Error uploading
dispute` and rolls back the disputed-fork marker ([#L264-L273](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L264-L273)), so nothing is uploaded and
    the peer may sign again. `canConstructMoreEvidence` answers `undefined` only for
    `PartialAuditingDataError` ([#L405](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L405)); any other construction error rejects the comparison,
    which is then not kept (decision 12). Why: the proof start rule and the walk have one owner
    each (`AgreementManager`, the contracts' canonical walk); a second check here would duplicate
    them. A proof built from a local start older than the chain's start stays acceptable, because
    the chain drops milestones wholly below its start. Only a full self-audit of the own dispute
    would catch other faults (Missing behavior)
    ([`REQ-DISPUTE-PIPE-13-W73B2F` (Proof construction from the local start)](../../../../specification/disputes/dispute-processing.md#req-dispute-pipe-13-w73b2f)).
15. **Auditing data reads the chain's walk and stays byte-equal to the disputer's.** `getAuditingData`
    takes a `BuiltStateProof` or a foreign `StateProof` ([#L779-L788](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L779-L788)). A foreign proof is
    described with `agreementManager.describeStateProof` (proof start and one snapshot per
    milestone, no walk) ([#L798-L802](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L798-L802)). A milestone snapshot the node does not hold sets
    `isPartial`, with the genesis snapshot as a length placeholder ([#L805-L809](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L805-L809)). When nothing
    is partial yet, the finalized snapshot comes from one read of the chain's walk
    (`stateChannelManagerContract.verifyMilestones.staticCall`), for a built proof and for a foreign
    proof alike: a valid walk gives its finalized snapshot, an invalid walk gives none, and a read
    error throws out of `getAuditingData` ([#L810-L826](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L810-L826)). A partial rebuild reads no walk. The
    builder's own local walk (`BuiltStateProof.finalizedSnapshot`) is not used here.
    `latestFinalizedStateStateMachineState` is the stored state of that finalized snapshot, `"0x"`
    when there is none (the walk rejected), and `""` with `isPartial` when the state is not stored
    ([#L843-L852](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L843-L852)). Why: the chain's `verifyStateProof` binds the posted finalized state to the
    snapshot its own walk ends at, and the local mirror can lag the chain's start; a state
    picked from a local walk would then be the state of a lower snapshot, and an honest disputer's
    posted data would fail the chain's proof verification. The auditing data hash is committed in the dispute, so every honest
    rebuilder must write the same bytes; `"0x"` is the one value every rebuilder writes for a
    rejected walk. A failed read is an internal failure, not missing data: it is fatal and never
    becomes a partial or a guessed value. A partial rebuild reads no outbound range either: its
    substituted genesis would give wrong bounds, as for the inbound run.

The posted-calldata handler implements [`REQ-DISPUTE-PIPE-11-HRGJ43` (Release a timeout refused for posted calldata)](../../../../specification/disputes/dispute-processing.md#req-dispute-pipe-11-hrgj43): inside the handler, still holding the dispute mutex, drop the stored candidate by identity so a waiter behind the mutex rebuilds without it; after the marker is released, read the posted block for that slot from the local calldata store and ingest it once. Every other failed submission hands back the stored posted block for the fork's next slot the same way ([DisputeManager.ts L292-L313](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L292-L313)), read under the state mutex; only the posted-calldata refusal drops a candidate. Nothing is re-armed and nothing is resent — the pipeline's verdict on that block decides what follows. The early-chain-timestamp handler implements [`REQ-DISPUTE-PIPE-10-BT8YAR` (Recheck an early timeout submission)](../../../../specification/disputes/dispute-processing.md#req-dispute-pipe-10-bt8yar): retain the submitted timeout slot, derive the retry delay from the decoded error, roll back the marker, release the mutex, then call ParticipantTimeoutService.scheduleCheck. WindowCreatedTooEarly remains a no-op and unrelated errors keep their existing policy apart from that hand-back.

## Inputs, outputs, state, and side effects

| Aspect         | Contents                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Inputs         | `forkId` to escalate; a `Dispute` to kill; construction reads from block/snapshot/state/timeout/force-exit/inbound-message/fraud-proof storage, the agreement manager's built state proof (`buildStateProof`; for a foreign proof `describeStateProof`), the chain's walk of the proof (`verifyMilestones`) for the finalized state, and the local mirror's slash set. |
| Outputs        | `ConstructDisputeResult` (dispute, signed confirmation, auditing data, proofs-to-apply); transactions to the manager contract (batched proofs+upload, plain upload with/without calldata, dispute-fraud-proof application).                                                                                                                                            |
| Owned state    | The instance mutex; wiring fields (`channelId`, hooks); the memoized `getStateTransitionReplayGas` read (dropped on failure); `evidenceChecks`, at most one evidence-comparison promise per disputed fork (decision 12). No protocol state of its own.                                                                                                                 |
| Storage writes | `disputedForks` flag (set on submit, rolled back on failure) — everything else it touches is read-only here.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Side effects   | On-chain submissions; `onInitiatingDispute` hook; structured logs. The one protocol signature produced is the disputer's own signature over the encoded dispute ([`REQ-ID-3-KR0BE3`](../../../../specification/protocol-model/identity.md#req-id-3-kr0be3): local decision, never remote-triggered).                                                                   |

[`requestDispute`](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L328) collects the original attempt for diagnostics and rethrows an escaping rejection
on a detached promise branch. That branch enters the existing owning-context unhandled-rejection
funnel even when a collector observes the original promise. There is no log-only handling. Direct
awaited calls retain the original rejection; expected refusals still use normal recovery first.

## Assumptions, dependencies, trust boundaries, and limits

- **Callers are trusted local components** (event handler, state manager, reduction executor); no
  remote input reaches this file directly. Its outputs, however, become adversarial input to the
  chain — everything it submits is re-verified on-chain.
- **Storage-backed construction:** correctness of a constructed dispute presumes the storage
  modules' fidelity ([`REQ-IX-9-AV56NR` (Storage fidelity)](../../../../specification/interactions.md#req-ix-9-av56nr)) and the
  agreement manager's proof assembly; a state-hash mismatch between snapshot and stored state is
  treated as a fatal race ([#L611-L618](../../../../../../src/disputeManager/DisputeManager.ts#L611-L618)).
- **Residual (finalized state read at construction):** the chain's walk is read when the dispute is
  built. A same-fork snapshot post that lands inside the last milestone between construction and
  upload moves the chain's start, so the posted finalized state no longer matches the snapshot the
  chain's walk ends at and the honest dispute fails the chain's proof verification while its fork is
  not yet disputed (decision 15). Deferred: [`OQ-SPEC-POSTED-STATE-RACE-1-1ZM6XN` (Posted finalized state after a snapshot lands inside the last milestone)](../../../../specification/open-questions.md#oq-spec-posted-state-race-1-1zm6xn).
- **Mirror freshness:** slash-subset and finality probes read the mirror; staleness is bounded by
  the decisions above (eager kill recording; on-chain re-checks by the contract itself). Wrong-kill
  safety does not rest here — the auditor checks proof validity before storing a dispute fraud proof.
- **Ordering/concurrency:** `dispute()` is mutex-serialized per instance; `killDispute` is
  intentionally unserialized (decision 6). Transaction nonce, fee and gas management is the
  signer's (estimate plus headroom, decision 8); replay sends take that estimate plus the manager's
  replay gas (decision 11). The evidence memo is shared by concurrent audits
  and changed only by promise-identity checks (decision 12).
- **Residual (block gas limit):** nothing caps the replay send's gas limit at the block gas
  limit. The limit is the estimate with the signer's 50% headroom plus the replay requirement. With
  a searching estimator the estimate already contains the requirement, so the limit is at least
  2.5 × the requirement; with poker's budgets it still fits a 30M block, but a consumer whose
  transition budget exceeds roughly 9M does not fit. A send above the block gas limit fails at
  submission, never with a wrong verdict; the fraud then stays unproven on-chain. No send test
  against a searching 30M-block node exists. Open as [`FIND-GASEST-2-94YFZ6`](../../../../audit/open-findings.md#find-gasest-2-94yfz6).
- **Residual (evidence memo):** a fork that never gets a reduced-result event keeps its memo entry
  for the runtime's life (one promise per such fork). The once-per-fork answer relies on reduction
  merging evidence monotonically; a reducer change that broke monotonicity would make a later
  dispute's comparison differ from the first one's.
- **Limits:** one dispute per fork per instance lifetime (until rollback); auditing-data size bounded
  by the stream ranges between the proof start, latest, and finalized snapshots.
