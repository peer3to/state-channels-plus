## Responsibility and observable boundary

Owns synchronization proof replay and uncommitted live validation. Provable fraud aborts an uncommitted observer; committed peers delegate fraud and disputed-fork reactions to BlockValidationStrategy. Junk without a provable participant fault drops the sender without aborting.

## Key design decisions

The subjective hook accepts proved history. The constructor receives the live strategy; double-sign, invalid-transition, forged-inbound, objective-timestamp, known-genesis and disputed-fork hooks delegate to it for committed peers. See [SpectatingValidationStrategy.ts](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/validationStrategy/SpectatingValidationStrategy.ts#L242).

1. **The abort/drop split is the fail-closed rule:** provable fraud → stop following; unattributable junk → keep spectating ([`INV-SYNC-3-A7A2ED`](../../../../../specification/peer-communication/synchronization.md#inv-sync-3-a7a2ed) consumer side).
