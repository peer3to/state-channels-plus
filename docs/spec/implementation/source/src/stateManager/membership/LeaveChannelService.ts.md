# LeaveChannelService.ts

> **Source:** [LeaveChannelService.ts](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/membership/LeaveChannelService.ts) > **Design views:** [architecture.md](../../../../views/architecture/sdk/architecture.md) > **Status:** Authored implementation report.

## Responsibility and observable boundary

This service owns one terminal leave operation for a state manager. It captures the participant count and
fork at entry, sets self-removal before dispute input construction, substitutes the local leave-turn hook,
counts committed blocks, owns the local watchdog, and resolves only after local removal plus either the
settled snapshot or the on-chain reduced-fork commitment that removed the signer.
It does not author application exit calldata or dispose the outer runtime.

## Key design decisions

`onExitFallbackFailed` ([#L153](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/membership/LeaveChannelService.ts#L153)) acts only on an existing `exit-authored` operation on the same fork; missing operations, awaiting-exit operations and stale fork notifications are ignored. A `RaceConditionDisputeEvidencePeriodExpired` refusal moves the operation to `awaiting-settlement` ([#L166](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/membership/LeaveChannelService.ts#L166)): the chain raises it only for a window that already holds commitments, so that window's reduction reaches `onSettledStateObserved`, which resolves the leave or re-arms it on the successor fork. Every other fallback error rejects the promise without changing the watchdog phase.

A confirmed dispute settlement short-circuits before chain membership reads: when the operation awaited settlement or this runtime disputed the leave's fork, and the runtime has moved to another fork, the reduction is the proof of removal ([#L185](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/membership/LeaveChannelService.ts#L185)). Explicit runtime disposal rejects pending leave and does not wait for an in-flight dispute upload. The upload may finish later; its original rejection remains in the detached diagnostic collector, while the leave failure route cannot resettle the disposed operation. Graceful exit uses leaveChannel; disposal is local shutdown. See [LeaveChannelService.ts](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/membership/LeaveChannelService.ts#L185).

The watchdog and block bound route fallback errors through the same observer to fail the leave operation ([#L147](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/membership/LeaveChannelService.ts#L147)). Settlement reads chain membership only after local removal at a settled status, and only without an observed dispute settlement ([#L193](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/membership/LeaveChannelService.ts#L193)). The production watchdog remains 15 seconds, intentionally allowing fallback before a healthy exit turn. See [#L338](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/membership/LeaveChannelService.ts#L338).

`onSettledStateObserved` accepts `NOT_OPENED` as a settled status as well as `SYNCED` ([#L193](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/membership/LeaveChannelService.ts#L193)). When the last exits close the channel, `EventHandler.handleChannelClose` sets `NOT_OPENED` before this observer runs, so a check for `SYNCED` alone never resolved a leave whose exit closed the channel. A closed status is not enough by itself: the signer must still be absent locally, and the chain must no longer list it unless a dispute settlement that removed it was observed ([#L196](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/membership/LeaveChannelService.ts#L196)).

The watchdog and block-bound fallback (`startDisputeFallback`, [#L357](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/membership/LeaveChannelService.ts#L357)) treats a `RaceConditionDisputeEvidencePeriodExpired` refusal of its self-removal dispute the same way ([#L394](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/membership/LeaveChannelService.ts#L394)): it logs `Terminal channel leave waiting for the next dispute window`, moves to `awaiting-settlement`, and returns ([#L401](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/membership/LeaveChannelService.ts#L401)). The window still reduces, and on the next fork `onSettledStateObserved` re-arms `awaiting-exit` ([#L207](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/membership/LeaveChannelService.ts#L207)) and the leave disputes again. Every other refusal still rejects the leave. This resolves [`FIND-LEAVE-4-WDH0XC`](../../../../../audit/open-findings.md#find-leave-4-wdh0xc).

A leave of a `PENDING_PARTICIPANT` that has not observed its own join on chain enters `awaiting-join` ([#L252](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/membership/LeaveChannelService.ts#L252)): there is nothing to remove yet, and the chain can still admit the join until its authorization deadline. `scheduleJoinWait` ([#L275](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/membership/LeaveChannelService.ts#L275)) schedules the task `terminal channel leave join wait` for when the chain should be past that deadline (`deadline + 1` minus the local clock, at least `minimumSeconds`). When the join is observed or seated during the wait, `onOwnJoinObserved` ([#L263](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/membership/LeaveChannelService.ts#L263)) moves to `awaiting-exit` and arms the watchdog. `onSettledStateObserved` ignores `awaiting-join` ([#L179](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/membership/LeaveChannelService.ts#L179)), so a reduction or snapshot observed during the wait cannot settle an unlanded join early. When the task runs, `settleUnobservedJoin` ([#L314](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/membership/LeaveChannelService.ts#L314)) asks `MembershipService.getOwnJoinState`, the single owner of the join decision ([#L320](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/membership/LeaveChannelService.ts#L320)): `open` waits again for at least one second ([#L322](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/membership/LeaveChannelService.ts#L322)); `landed` continues as a member's leave (`awaiting-exit` plus watchdog, [#L326](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/membership/LeaveChannelService.ts#L326)); `expired` or `none` calls `abandonUnobservedJoin`, which returns the runtime to `SYNCED`, and resolves the leave without a dispute ([#L331](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/membership/LeaveChannelService.ts#L331)). `isAwaitingJoin` ([#L297](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/membership/LeaveChannelService.ts#L297)) is checked before and after the chain read, so an observation, a replacement operation, or disposal (`disposed`, [#L217](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/membership/LeaveChannelService.ts#L217)) during the read wins over the read. A read that throws rejects the leave through the detached observer. Rejected alternatives: disputing at once, which would self-remove a signer the chain may not list; settling after one turn window, which could drop a join that still lands and leave a funded membership with no runtime.

Committed membership uses the shared status predicate. Local and chain signer membership use MembershipService; leave sequencing, settlement and error wrapping remain local. See [LeaveChannelService.ts](../../../../../../../src/stateManager/membership/LeaveChannelService.ts#L97).

One stored promise makes leave idempotent and gates new channel work. The first `N + 1`-block or watchdog
bound starts the existing dispute path. A dispute already active on the entry fork is awaited; after settlement,
the operation either completes if the signer is absent or rearms on the next active fork. Disposal cancels the
watchdog and rejects an unfinished operation.

## Inputs, outputs, state, and side effects

LeaveChannelState exposes the operation phase, fork and captured bound through the state getter. The operation owns its promise, watchdog handle and progress; failure rejects that promise and disposal cancels unfinished work.

The public leave operation coordinates committed membership, outbound removal, receipt and completion. Its existing operation promise and hooks prevent duplicate work; results and status changes retain their lifecycle order.

## Assumptions, dependencies, trust boundaries, and limits

Only pending and participating states count as committed membership. The extracted status predicate does not change snapshot membership, channel pins or receipt/abort behavior.

## Specification adherence

The service covers the local state-machine part of terminal leave. Snapshot posting, dispute construction,
chain observation, port transport, and outer disposal remain with their existing owners.
