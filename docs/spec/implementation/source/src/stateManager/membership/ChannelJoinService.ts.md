## UNIT-TEST-CHANNEL-JOIN-SERVICE-1-BJKHEB

Own join, top-up and force join

- Setup: Drive first join, force-join eligibility, pending/participating reuse, and top-up through public signer paths; around a real chain snapshot move, hold or fail submissions, receipts, threshold countersignatures and the chain snapshot read; hold or delay the JOIN observation and the evidence window; hold fresh retry collection across the old deadline, stale queued checks and disposal; hold an expiry check after its chain-membership read answered absent, or run one while the refused submission's retry-eligibility read is held; fire a held expiry timer while the joiner's clock is still at or before the deadline; land a concurrent join first so the threshold set widens, and land a second one while the retry is held, also on a channel that second join fills; refuse a join or a top-up on a moved snapshot at every submission on a channel with a small maximum participant count; hold a full-channel refusal's retry decision while the snapshot moves to a stored one; submit a prepared join to a channel a landed pending join fills; connect with a join, with and without the spectate-when-full option, to a channel whose founders already fill it, or that a landed pending join fills; land a spectator's join while a top-up is held at its send; fail a join's or a top-up's first submission with a mined receipt that carries no revert data while the replay of the same call passes on the real chain, and hold the submission again after its estimate while another join lands; abort the runtime while a top-up's collected fresh confirmation is held; overlap two connect joins on one synced spectator; connect with a join, and a participating member's top-up, while one threshold member holds its countersignature past the request deadline; fail a connect join's threshold set read once; request a leave through the public `leaveChannel` before the own force-join dispute and remove the join from the local state and the chain before or after that dispute; overlap two top-up calls; dispose during a held join check or a held force-join deadline setup, with a record-only scheduling probe
- Oracle: Pending starts before contract invocation; only final proven no-commitment failure ends the logical join `SYNCED`; the internal retry preserves pending status and promise without an intermediate onLeftChannel: the refused record is retired before the retry-eligibility read, so a queued check stops at its entry and a check already past an awaited read stops at its re-check of the active record before it drops the membership, records an observation or arms a timer; an expiry check that runs while this Clock is not past the deadline arms the expiry timer again, so an expired unobserved join that the chain does not list ends `SYNCED`; force join waits for on-chain membership and a usable dispute window and starts one dispute at the exact block bound or deadline; committed reuse without a balance sends zero transactions, and a join or top-up sends one more submission only after a proven failure while the chain snapshot moved from the latest submission's stored pin to a stored snapshot or after a refusal for a threshold set a landed join widened, collected again at once; a mined refusal with no revert data is only replayed (no transaction is sent by the replay): a replay that names a refusal is judged as that refusal, and a replay that passes sends the same confirmation again with no fresh collection, as one more retry under the same cap; each such refusal of a retry is retried again, up to the channel's maximum participant count of retries, past which the refusal is final; a refusal for a full channel is never retried, even when the snapshot moved after the refusal; a connect's join to a channel whose seated and pending participants the chain shows full requests no countersignature and submits nothing, while a prepared join makes no such read and is submitted; a join call while one runs gets that join's promise; a full channel, found before collecting or refused by the chain, aborts the runtime by default and leaves it `SYNCED` with no abort under the spectate-when-full option; a runtime disposed while a submission is pending handles no refusal of it, so no status change after the abort's `OPENED`; overlapping top-up calls share one submission; a failed attempt is told apart from a funded success, and a successful retry credits the requested amount exactly once; a first join's collection that fails with `JoinCollectionError` submits nothing, returns `false` and aborts the runtime, and a top-up's returns `false` with its status kept and no abort; any other collection error rejects the call with no abort and no submission; a fresh collection that fails with `JoinCollectionError` ends the call `false` with no further submission, and a runtime disposed during a top-up's fresh collection submits nothing more; an observed join that both the local state and the chain omit ends `SYNCED` with one onLeftChannel and no dispute of its own after the drop; a check released after disposal sends no dispute, emits no membership event and schedules no join timer; a terminal-revert abort shows the restored `SYNCED` as a status transition, because the abort's own `OPENED` would otherwise mask it
