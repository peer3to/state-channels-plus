## Purpose and observable contract

Block confirmations (a signed block plus its collected confirmation signatures) spread by gossip:
every participating node that authors, counter-signs, or learns new signatures re-broadcasts the grown
confirmation to every open session, fire-and-forget. This service is the receiving end — a
deliberately thin attribution shim: **the communication layer owns caller admission, sender
attribution, and the verdict-to-consequence mapping; the pipeline owns every judgment about the
bytes.** The service performs no protocol validation of the payload.

## Algorithm

**Sending.** Participating peers relay accepted growth of blocks whose participant union contains them. Spectators, pending joiners, and a leaver whose exit is pending persist it without relaying. After a confirmation-changing event (authoring, counter-signing, signature merge
growth), broadcast the updated confirmation to all open sessions. No delivery receipt is expected;
gossip redundancy plus the recovery paths of [`REQ-BLOCK-PIPE-4-CF52J6` (Recovery without bypass)](../block-progression/block-processing.md#req-block-pipe-4-cf52j6) provide eventual delivery.

**Receiving.**

1. The frame passes ingress dispatch and the authenticated-session gate ([rpc.md](./rpc.md)).
2. While the receiving runtime is not yet synced, the frame waits under [`REQ-GOSSIP-5-2EQ7P6`](block-gossip.md#req-gossip-5-2eq7p6) and continues from this step once the runtime is synced.
3. The sender's proven identity is attached to the confirmation as source attribution — the
   attribution that intake preserves per [`REQ-BLOCK-PIPE-1-SS24D1` (Unified work item)](../block-progression/block-processing.md#req-block-pipe-1-ss24d1) and the queue stores per
   <a id="req-qstore-1-ps769j"></a>`REQ-QSTORE-1-PS769J`.
4. The attributed confirmation is handed to pipeline intake ([`REQ-IX-1-WTJ0D1` (Peer block ingress)](../interactions.md#req-ix-1-wtj0d1)).
   Intake authenticates the author and channel, then admits the source under [`REQ-GOSSIP-4-J5Z4DF`](block-gossip.md#req-gossip-4-j5z4df) before queue insertion or stored merging. Intake is the merge regime: unordered, duplicable, mutex-free ([`REQ-BLOCK-PIPE-5-WJ31RG` (Pre-execution merge layer)](../block-progression/block-processing.md#req-block-pipe-5-wj31rg)).
5. The pipeline's verdict maps back to a communication-layer consequence: acceptable knowledge
   (new, duplicate, mergeable, not-yet-eligible) has none; an objective protocol violation
   attributable to the sender terminates and excludes the sender; non-attributable junk is dropped
   with at most session-level consequences.

## Failure outcomes

| Failure                                                        | Outcome                                                                   |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Unauthenticated or malformed frame                             | Ingress dispatch consequences ([rpc.md](./rpc.md)); never reaches intake. |
| Duplicate/older/future confirmation                            | No penalty — merged or held by the pipeline's rules.                      |
| Sender-attributable objective violation (per pipeline verdict) | Terminate and exclude the sender.                                         |
| Non-attributable invalid data                                  | Drop; session-level consequence at most.                                  |
| Live confirmation before the receiver is synced                | Retained, then processed by normal intake once synced; dropped on abort.  |

## Requirements and invariants

**<a id="req-gossip-1-htk3nx"></a>`REQ-GOSSIP-1-HTK3NX` — Thin attributed ingress.** The gossip service MUST attach the authenticated
sender identity to every received confirmation and hand it to pipeline intake unmodified; it MUST
NOT validate, filter, reorder, or merge protocol content itself.

**<a id="req-gossip-2-9pmmnh"></a>`REQ-GOSSIP-2-9PMMNH` — Verdict-mapped consequences.** Communication-layer penalties for gossiped content
MUST follow the pipeline's verdict classification; the gossip layer never penalizes content the
pipeline classifies as acceptable knowledge, and never forgives what the pipeline attributes as the
sender's own violation.

**<a id="req-gossip-3-hqznqx"></a>`REQ-GOSSIP-3-HQZNQX` — Re-broadcast on growth.** A participating node MUST re-broadcast accepted signature growth for blocks whose participant union contains it. Spectators and pending joiners MUST persist accepted growth without unsolicited block relaying. A participant that has left MUST relay its leave block and MUST NOT relay blocks committed after it while its exit is pending: peers that already applied its exit no longer admit it as a source. Participant promotion restores normal gossip. Duplicate copies produce no rebroadcast. Receiving, applying, retaining confirmations, and requesting or serving sync remain allowed.

**[`REQ-QSTORE-1-PS769J`](block-gossip.md#req-qstore-1-ps769j) — Copy-scoped contribution history.** Intake MUST preserve which authenticated source supplied each admitted signature. Contributions from other copies MUST NOT be attributed to a sender that did not supply them. The [queue contract](../storage/queue.md) defines independent source allowances and ordinary queue processing.

**<a id="req-gossip-4-j5z4df"></a>`REQ-GOSSIP-4-J5Z4DF` — Eligible transport contribution.** Authenticate the transport identity separately from the block author. After cheap structure, channel, and author-authentication checks, admit network contributions only from a sender in either the on-chain current/pending eligibility cache or the latest verified current-fork participant-union cache. Known on-chain slashes override both. A positive cache lookup is constant-time and performs no new chain or state-machine read. Apply this gate to stored duplicates too.

When both caches miss, refresh authoritative chain membership and recheck eligibility. If the sender
is still absent, await ordinary sync for the block's channel, fork and minimum height. After it returns,
recheck cached eligibility regardless of the sync result and blacklist a sender that is not eligible. Then end the request. Do not queue or merge the triggering copy after sync. Verified history is applied by the
existing sync pipeline. Its caller waits for applicable in-flight work. Sync distinguishes invalid
payloads from request availability failures under the synchronization rules. A failed chain read rejects the refresh under the [SDK-wide RPC availability assumption](../runtime/sdk.md#assumptions-and-constraints); it does not continue to the ordinary sync path as an unanswered refresh. A completed chain search that does not return a needed log is an unanswered refresh, not an RPC outage.
There is no separate candidate registry, admission timer, retry window or post-sync processing of the triggering copy.

Publish cache replacements atomically after verified state application or commit. Do not publish failed,
cancelled, or dispute-replay state as current membership. Maintain three sets: chain members, verified local members, and observed slashes.
Lookups query those sets directly. Verified commits and state application replace the local set;
channel selection clears the sets.
Observed membership events push their data into the chain mirror without membership reads. Snapshots replace current membership while preserving known unconsumed joins. On a miss, explicitly fetch missing chain state in one read pinned at one chain block and apply it through the same observation pipeline so both membership and execution mirrors are updated. The fetch applies the whole pending inbound run (after the on-chain snapshot's consumed inbound head, up to the channel's inbound head). Applying a block the mirror already holds is a no-op whatever source applied it first (a live event, event recovery or an earlier fetch), and a block the mirror misses below its head is filled in. The fetch applies its snapshot at the chain position of its pinned read, after every event of that block, so a later snapshot event the mirror already applied is kept, and membership keeps it too: a snapshot the mirror drops as older updates no membership. Applying the fetch makes no chain read that the pinned read already answers. A refresh whose chain search does not return a log it needs has no answer; any failure during the refresh, a chain read or a local state application, is fatal;
observed slashes take precedence over late reads. Positive caches are not polled per frame: an unobserved
chain change can remain temporarily stale. Refreshes reuse observed-state publication without generation checks or
automatic retry loops. Concurrent misses share one in-flight refresh; completion or failure releases it for a later miss. Current transport admission is separate from the block's
historical signer union and from on-chain dispute standing.

**<a id="req-gossip-5-2eq7p6"></a>`REQ-GOSSIP-5-2EQ7P6` — Live gossip waits for the initial sync.** A live block
confirmation that an authenticated peer sends while the receiving runtime is not yet synced (its status is
before `SYNCED`, `PENDING_PARTICIPANT` or `PARTICIPATING`) MUST be retained, not dropped. A retained
confirmation MUST NOT advance state, enter the queue, or start a second sync or a sync probe while it waits.
Once the runtime is synced, each retained confirmation MUST be processed in arrival order through the same
admission, normal validation and fork handling as a confirmation that arrives then, without the sender
resending it; a stale or wrong-fork confirmation is therefore judged, not applied. Retention MUST NOT block
the initial sync, which applies its own verified history outside live gossip. A runtime that aborts drops
what it retains and processes none of it. A retained frame gets no failure response.

## Assumptions and constraints

- Highest-volume ingress surface of the node; per-peer rate bounds ([`REQ-RPC-5-CV1R1Y` (Resource bounds)](rpc.md#req-rpc-5-cv1r1y),
  [`OQ-6-4JPNE5` (P2P gossip rate limiting)](../open-questions.md#oq-6-4jpne5)) are the intended admission control — the pipeline's queue
  deliberately relies on this layer for frequency bounding.
- Fire-and-forget delivery: loss is recovered by gossip redundancy and pipeline sync, not by this
  service.
- Live confirmations that arrive before the receiver is synced wait in memory until the initial sync settles
  or the runtime aborts ([`REQ-GOSSIP-5-2EQ7P6`](block-gossip.md#req-gossip-5-2eq7p6)). Retention has no count bound of its own beyond the
  per-peer rate bounds above.
- Full-mesh fan-out cost is bounded by the partition-size limit
  ([`REQ-TRUST-5-NDVRW8`](../security/trust-model.md#req-trust-5-ndvrw8)).

## Security considerations

The service's safety depends on _not_ being clever: any validation or filtering here would create a
second, weaker judgment of protocol content that an adversary could play against the pipeline's
([`REQ-GOSSIP-1-HTK3NX`](block-gossip.md#req-gossip-1-htk3nx) prevents divergence). Attribution fidelity is the security payload — it converts
flooding and forgery into attributable evidence downstream. Flooding is the main residual until
rate limiting lands; structural caps in the queue bound per-entry damage. Retention before sync
([`REQ-GOSSIP-5-2EQ7P6`](block-gossip.md#req-gossip-5-2eq7p6)) adds no authority: a retained frame passes the same admission and validation later, and a
flood sent during an initial sync is held only until that sync settles or aborts.
